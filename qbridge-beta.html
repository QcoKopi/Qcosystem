Beyond Traceability: Digital Fairness, Cultural Capital, and Trust in Indonesian Coffee

Abstract: This paper presents a novel framework for trust infrastructure in Indonesian coffee supply chains. Drawing on four years of market intelligence from US specialty coffee exhibitions (SCE/WOC 2022–2026) and diplomatic trade partnerships (KBRI Washington, 2024–2026), we propose a three-layer verification system that moves beyond traditional traceability to incorporate digital fairness — the transparent distribution of value across supply chain actors — and cultural capital — the recognition of indigenous processing knowledge as a tradable asset.

The QBridge model combines administrative verification (legal entity, certifications), physical processing validation (Q Processing standards, equipment inspection, SOP compliance), and independent quality assessment (Q Grader cupping with CVA scoring). A welfare scoring component evaluates labor practices, farmer relationships, and sustainability metrics, creating a composite trust signal for global buyers.

We argue that trust in agricultural commodities cannot be reduced to geographic tracking alone. Instead, it requires the integration of institutional verification, technical competence, and ethical governance — what we term a "trust infrastructure" layer. This framework has been validated through field research with Indonesian processors and buyer interviews across North American and European markets.

The System

Three Layers. One Trust Signal.

No other platform verifies Indonesian coffee processors across administrative, technical, and quality dimensions simultaneously. Each layer is independent. Together, they create a composite badge that global buyers can trust.

1

Administrative Verification

Validator: Document Reviewer

Legal entity verification, business registration, tax compliance, and certification audit.

  • NIB (Nomor Induk Berusaha) validation
  • NPWP & tax compliance check
  • SIUP/TDP verification
  • Organic, Fair Trade, ISO certification audit
  • Business address & contact verification
2

Physical & Process Validation

Validator: Q Processing Certified

On-site facility inspection, equipment audit, SOP evaluation, and processing method verification.

  • Pulper, demucilager, huller inspection
  • Suton, size grader, color sorter audit
  • SOP (Standard Operating Procedure) review
  • All methods: washed, natural, honey, wet-hulled, anaerobic
  • Mill condition & hygiene assessment
3

Quality Assessment

Validator: Q Grader (CQI)

Independent cupping with CVA (Coffee Value Assessment) scoring, defect analysis, and physical grading.

  • CVA scoring: Physical, Descriptive, Affective, Extrinsic
  • Defect count & classification
  • Moisture content measurement
  • Density & screen size analysis
  • Cupping notes & flavor profile documentation

FAQ

Common Questions.

Is QBridge a marketplace? Do you handle transactions? +
No. QBridge is an information provider — a trust infrastructure layer. We verify processors and make their profiles available to buyers. All transactions, negotiations, and contracts happen directly between the buyer and processor. We do not take commissions on trade.
What does verification cost for processors? +
Beta participants join free of charge. Post-launch, verification fees will be structured to cover validator costs (Q Processing inspection, Q Grader cupping) without creating a barrier for small and medium processors. Detailed pricing will be published before public launch.
How is blockchain traceability implemented? +
Each verified batch receives a unique blockchain record documenting origin, processing method, quality scores, and chain of custody. Buyers can scan a QR code on the processor profile to verify batch authenticity. The system is designed for transparency, not speculation — we use a permissioned ledger optimized for supply chain integrity.
What is the welfare scoring system? +
Welfare scoring evaluates labor practices (no child labor, no forced labor), worker safety, environmental stewardship, and farmer-supplier relationships. A score ≥ 90 is required for Platinum badge status. This aligns with ESG investing criteria and buyer sustainability mandates.
