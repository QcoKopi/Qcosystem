## PRESENTATION NOTES

### Timing
- **First pass** (email / mobile): 3 minutes. Investor skims. Title, Problem, Solution, Traction, Ask must be visible in 3 minutes.
- **Second pass** (desktop / deep dive): 10 minutes. Investor reads details. Team, Market, Business Model, Competitive Landscape matter here.
- **Live pitch**: 15-20 minutes + 15 minutes Q&A. Practice until you can deliver in 12 minutes comfortably.

### Q&A Preparation
Anticipate these questions and have 1-slide answers ready:

1. **"How do you prevent competitors from copying your 3-layer verification?"**
   - Answer: Academic moat (ICC paper establishes prior art). Network effects (more verified processors = harder to replicate). Diplomatic moat (KBRI relationship). Founder expertise (Q Grader + Q Processing certification is not easily replicated).

2. **"What if processors don't want to pay for verification?"**
   - Answer: Beta is free. Post-launch, pricing is tiered and subsidized for smallholders. The value proposition is clear: verified processors get higher prices and direct buyer access. Q.co Lab proves buyers pay premium for verified quality.

3. **"How do you scale validator capacity?"**
   - Answer: Partnership with CQI Indonesia. Train-the-trainer model. Remote validation tools for administrative layer. Physical inspection requires travel but is only needed once per processor (annual recertification).

4. **"Why GAS instead of 'real' backend technology?"**
   - Answer: Asset-light, zero CapEx, rapid iteration. We can deploy features in hours. At scale, we migrate to Firebase/Node.js — architecture is modular, no vendor lock-in. Current stack is a competitive advantage, not a limitation.

5. **"What is your exit strategy?"**
   - Answer: Strategic acquisition by global coffee trader (e.g., Neumann, Volcafe, Sucafina) seeking verified supply chain infrastructure. Or IPO as Indonesia's first agritech infrastructure platform. Timeline: 7-10 years.

6. **"How much are you raising, and what is the valuation?"**
   - Answer: [FILL BEFORE PRESENTING]. Pre-money valuation based on Q.co Lab revenue, platform development cost, IP value, and comparable pre-seed agritech deals in Southeast Asia.

7. **"What is the biggest risk to your timeline?"**
   - Answer: Validator recruitment for QBridge launch. Mitigated by CQI partnership and existing network. Secondary risk: buyer acquisition speed. Mitigated by 4 years of SCE/WOC relationships and ICC Italy exposure.
