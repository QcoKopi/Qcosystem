The Opportunity

A Fragmented Market. A Verified Solution.

The Problem

  • Indonesian farmers lose 60% of potential income to opaque middlemen
  • Global buyers pay 3x markup for traceable specialty coffee
  • No trust infrastructure exists for Indonesian processors — buyers rely on personal networks, not verified data
  • 4th largest producer globally, yet <2% direct trade penetration
  • ESG-minded buyers cannot verify farmer welfare or processing standards

The QBridge Solution

  • Layer 1 — Admin: NIB, NPWP, legal entity, certifications verified
  • Layer 2 — Physical: Q Processing (QP1/QP2) facility inspection, equipment, SOP
  • Layer 3 — Quality: Q Grader cupping with CVA scoring, defect analysis, moisture/density
  • Blockchain Traceability: Immutable records from farm to export
  • Welfare Scoring: ESG-aligned farmer and labor practice assessment
